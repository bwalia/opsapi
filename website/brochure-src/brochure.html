Before you watch

What you are evaluating

A tax return application for the UK with an AI agent built in. It reads bank statements (PDF or CSV), sorts each transaction into the right HMRC category, works out the figures, and files with HMRC through Making Tax Digital for Income Tax and for VAT.

The agent does the slow reading and sorting. People make the decisions: the client confirms the figures, an accountant can review any line, and nothing is sent to HMRC until someone ticks the declaration.

We sell it as a customised build: your branding, your categories and rules, your HMRC credentials, and an AI agent set up for your clients, hosted by us or on your own servers.

Every video shows

  • The working application, not slides or a mock-up
  • On-screen captions explaining each step
  • Long AI steps sped up, with the real time stated
  • HMRC's own consent screen and responses (sandbox)
  • Test people and test businesses only, no real taxpayer data

Scenario 03 · VAT · Making Tax Digital for VAT

Landlord VAT return: coded lines to the 9 boxes to HMRC's receipt

A VAT-registered property business codes every bank line with a VAT treatment, works out the quarter's 9-box return, opens any box to see the lines behind it, and submits it to HMRC. It then works out the annual partial-exemption adjustment and carries it to the next return.

The AI agent
Suggests a VAT rate for each line only after your own rules and past decisions, and flags lines that need a receipt or a second look.
The application
A rules engine calculates the 9 boxes, never the AI. It handles partial exemption (here, 45% of VAT on shared costs is reclaimed) and keeps HMRC's receipt.
For your evaluation
A VAT return you can audit: every box links to its lines, figures are downloadable as CSV, and HMRC's copy is kept beside yours.

What you get

Customised software with the AI agent set up for you

You choose the scenarios that match your business. We build them under your brand, set up the AI agent on your categories and rules, connect it to HMRC with your credentials, and run it with you until the first live returns are filed.

An app you offer your clients

Your name, domain and sign-in, as in scenario 06, with iPhone and Android apps in the enterprise licence. Clients use it in place of QuickBooks or Xero, email and Dropbox.

AI agent setup

The chat assistant, the statement reader and the transaction classifier, configured with your categories, your rules and your past decisions. It asks before guessing, and a person signs off.

HMRC Making Tax Digital

Income Tax Self Assessment (updates, calculation, final declaration) and VAT (9 boxes, partial exemption, annual adjustment). Includes HMRC's fraud-prevention headers and help getting your production credentials.

Hosting and data

Hosted in the UK by us, or on your own servers. Run the agent on a hosted model, or on a local model so bank statements never leave your network.

What it does not do

  • The AI never calculates the tax or the VAT boxes. A rules engine does, and HMRC confirms.
  • Nothing is filed without a person confirming the figures and ticking the declaration.
  • These videos use HMRC's sandbox. Live filing needs your HMRC production approval, which can take a few weeks.
  • HMRC does not accept a final declaration before the tax year ends. The app shows the estimate until then.
  • Speed depends on the model. On our test server, a local model took about two hours to read 12 monthly statements. A GPU or a hosted model is much faster.
