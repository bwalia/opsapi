# Compliance templates: not legal advice

The workflow templates and compliance checklists that ship with Property Deals are **editable
starting points**. They are not legal, regulatory or tax advice. They don't replace your own
solicitor, your compliance officer or your supervisory body.

- The UK seed template ("guaranteed-sale buyer + investor sourcing", England & Wales) lists common
  steps: AML customer due diligence under the Money Laundering Regulations 2017, sanctions and PEP
  screening, source of funds, redress-scheme membership, consumer-protection records, UK GDPR
  consent and retention, and conveyancing milestones. The list may be incomplete or out of date for
  your business, and it doesn't cover other jurisdictions (Scotland and Northern Ireland differ).
- **Before you use a template on real deals, your own solicitor or compliance lead must review it.**
  They should change stages, tasks, deadlines, gates and checks to fit your business, and approve
  it. Each change publishes a new template version, so you can show which version a deal followed.
- The system enforces the process your template describes. A person must sign off every compliance
  check, and nothing is sent without a named approval. **The system doesn't decide whether a check
  is legally sufficient.** AI agents may draft, compare and flag. They never pass a check or clear
  a legal issue.
- Keep your own records as your regulator requires. Use the data export and retention settings to
  meet your obligations.
